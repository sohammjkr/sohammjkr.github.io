\begin{document}

\lead{Ten months at GridTran, one office party, and a holiday weekend spread
across three states.}

The honest measure of how much the company has grown is the conference table.
When I started there was room to spread out at it. At the Fourth of July party
we were shoulder to shoulder, and I was still being introduced to people. The
family has grown considerably, and I do mean family.

\img{/assets/GridTranOfficeJuly4.jpg}{The office party, at a table that used to
feel a good deal larger.}

Holiday weekends are when the gratitude list gets its annual audit. This team
is near the top of mine. Ten months is long enough to know who you would want
next to you when something on the bench refuses to work at five in the
afternoon, and I have a full set of those people now.

\sep

My next stop was a short hop to DC to see my good buddy Neil. We had two
birthdays to get through, his 24th and America's 250th, and we did both in
style: rowdy games, better company, and food that would have justified the trip
on its own.

This is the only photo I took of the entire encounter, which tells you
everything you need to know about how the weekend was going.

\img[70]{/assets/DCWithNeil.jpg}{The sum total of my photographic record. Neil
driving, me not driving.}

Neil has also been arguing with me about the correct order of milk and cereal
since long before either of us had any business holding opinions. He is wrong.
He has always been wrong. It is one of the great constants of my life and I
would not trade it.

\sep

I rounded out the vacation with a quick trip back home to my family, where we
marked the occasion the way real Americans do, with a good cookout.

\img[50]{/assets/CaryGrillOut.jpg}{Marinated chicken, four sausages, one man
convinced he had it under control.}

A cookout in my family means somebody stands over the coals for an hour
insisting they do not need help while everyone else brings them things. The
chicken went on in two colors, pale and slightly less pale, and came off
correct. The sausages required no supervision and got plenty.

Back home Sunday night, slightly sunburnt, entirely full.
