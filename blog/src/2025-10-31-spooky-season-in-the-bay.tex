\title{Spooky Season in the Bay}
\date{2025-10-31 to 2025-11-03}
\location{San Francisco, CA}
\tags{travel, friends, food}

\begin{document}

\lead{The hardest part about leaving undergrad is that you cannot take your
friends with you. So I suppose you have to go to them.}

Enter Spooky Season. Quite impulsively, all of us booked tickets and met in San
Francisco for the weekend, Friday through Monday, on the reasoning that
Halloween landing on a Friday is not a circumstance you can ignore.

\img[65]{/assets/SFPaintedLadies.jpg}{Alamo Square, doing the obligatory thing.}

We went full tourist mode and made no apologies for it. Fisherman's Wharf.
Ghirardelli Square. The Stanford Quad. Matcha, brunch and boba, in quantities
I would be embarrassed by if I were the sort of person who gets embarrassed
about being a performative male.

\img{/assets/SFBrunch.jpg}{Brunch, the load-bearing meal of the entire trip.}

\img[65]{/assets/SFCrew.jpg}{The full crew}

And to round it out, a bar crawl on Halloween. We went as Alvin and the
Chipmunks. I will not be elaborating.

\sep

This one goes on the Mount Rushmore of trips for me. I will not forget it soon,
and I expect to miss it often.

\end{document}
