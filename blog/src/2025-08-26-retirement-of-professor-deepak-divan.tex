\title{The Retirement of Professor Deepak Divan}
\date{2025-08-26}
\location{Atlanta, GA}
\tags{life}

\begin{document}

\lead{A lifetime of impact in research and industry, and I got to be in the room
on the day we marked it.}

Start with the numbers, because they are faintly ridiculous. More than 80
patents. Nearly 35,000 citations. An IEEE Fellow and a member of the National
Academy of Engineering.

Then the companies he founded and ran: SoftSwitching Technologies, Varentec,
Innovolt, SmartWires, GridBlock, GigaGrid, GridTran. Those are only the ones I
know about.

\img[55]{/assets/DivanStairs.jpg}{Prof. Divan, mid-story.}

When people say power electronics has its legends, this is who they mean. That
is not a courtesy and it is not a stretch.

\sep

I am truly grateful to have been one of Prof. Divan's Master's students, and as
it turns out, his last one. A year of working with him has changed how I think
about the grid, about what is worth building, and about how quickly a good idea
should be put in front of something real.

\img{/assets/DivanCDESign.jpg}{At the Center for Distributed Energy.}

The send-off was the right size for the occasion. People came, ate, and told
stories about him, most of which he cheerfully corrected.

\img{/assets/DivanRetirementSpread.jpg}{Plates first, speeches later.}

\img[full]{/assets/DivanRetirementGroup.jpg}{Most of the people who turned up.
There were more.}

\sep

Dr. Divan has officially closed his door at GT and opened a new one at GT.
Georgia Tech on the way out, GridTran on the way in.

Thank you, Prof., for everything you have contributed to this field and for
everything you are still going to. I have learned an enormous amount in the
past year and I am looking forward to this next stint at GridTran, and to
continuing the fun.

The real work begins now!
