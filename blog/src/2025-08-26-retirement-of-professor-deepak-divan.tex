\title{The Retirement of Professor Deepak Divan}
\date{2025-08-26}
\location{Atlanta, GA}
\tags{life}

\begin{document}

\lead{A lifetime of impact in research and industry, and I got to be in the room
on the day we marked it.}

Start with the numbers, because they are faintly ridiculous. More than 80
patents. Nearly 35,000 citations. An IEEE Fellow and a member of the National
Academy of Engineering.

Then the companies he founded and ran: SoftSwitching Technologies, Varentec,
Innovolt, SmartWires, GridBlock, GigaGrid, GridTran. Those are only the ones I
know about.

\img[55]{/assets/DivanStairs.jpg}{Prof. Divan, mid-story.}

When people say power electronics has its legends, this is who they mean. They don't make em like this anymore.

\sep

I am truly grateful to have been one of Prof. Divan's students, and as
it turns out, his last Masters student. After only a year of working with him has changed how I think
about the urgent challenges that we face as power electronics engineers today. Prof. Divan's ability to contextualize engineering challenges within the broader energy landscape has taught me how to think holistically and the importance of identifying great problems to pursue greater solutions. His rigorous commitment to impactful research has set a standard that I will carry forward in my career.

\img{/assets/DivanCDESign.jpg}{At the Center for Distributed Energy.}

The send-off was a classy affair and just the right size for the occasion. People came, ate, and told
stories about him, there is a photo somewhere of the after party where around 15 former and current grad students were crowded around him as he told stories about the early days of power electronics, the state of academic research and where its come til now. I don't think you could pay those students enough to get them to leave while the inventor of the Dual Active Bridge was talking about inventing it.

\img{/assets/DivanRetirementSpread.jpg}{Plates first.}

\img[full]{/assets/DivanRetirementGroup.jpg}{Folks who have been part of Prof. Divan's story over the years.}

\sep

Dr. Divan has officially closed his door at GT (Georgia Tech) and opened a new one at GT (GridTran). 

Thank you, Prof., for everything you have contributed to this field and for everything you will do. I have learned an enormous amount in the past year and I am looking forward to this next stint at GridTran, and to continuing the fun.

The real work begins now!
