\title{The Lads do Curaçao}
\date{2026-01-17 to 2026-01-21}
\location{Curaçao}
\tags{travel, friends, food}

\begin{document}

\lead{Snorkeling straight off the beach, island roads, stroopwafels. What is
not to love about the ABC islands.}

\img[60]{/assets/CuracaoDeparture.jpg}{Departures, optimism fully intact.}

The trip summarizes cleanly: too much time in the pool and too much time in the
ocean. Both turn out to be good for the soul and for the spirit, and I have no
notes.

\img[70]{/assets/CuracaoCoast.jpg}{The water is genuinely that color. I checked
several times in case it was a trick.}

We drove the island roads, and I am putting in writing that we went the wrong
way down a one way for a while, so it counts as a confession rather than a
rumor. We also saw peacocks. Nobody had mentioned the peacocks. I have chosen
to file them as a bonus feature.

\img{/assets/CuracaoWillemstad.jpg}{Willemstad, which looks exactly like the
postcards for the excellent reason that the postcards are accurate.}

The food deserves its own sentence, so here it is: the food was really,
genuinely good, and I did not have one meal I would trade.

\img[70]{/assets/CuracaoSunset.jpg}{Last evening, doing the absolute most.}

Overall a great trip. We will do it again. At some point. Probably.
