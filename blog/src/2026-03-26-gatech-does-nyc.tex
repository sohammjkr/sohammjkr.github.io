\lead{Four and a half grad students larping as locals, dressed like the most
performative males you have ever seen.}

So begins the yearly pilgrimage. We go to the same sights, we eat at the same
places, and all of it lands anyway, because the city does not need to be new to
work on you.

\img[70]{/assets/NYCWelcomeManhattan.jpg}{The sign still says ``Hello, gorgeous!''
There is a new name under it now.}

First visit under the management of Zohran, which practically speaking meant
standing at a crosswalk reading a road sign out loud. Five of us under it, one
handshake, not one person looking at the camera. I said performative and I
meant it.

New York is always great. New York with these people is better, and that is
most of the reason the trip is annual.

\sep

\section{Broadway}

Fresh off the plane from ATL we hopped onto the bus with ALL of our stuff and
beelined it for the Lyric Theatre to catch our night show of \textit{Harry
Potter and the Cursed Child}, with Tom Felton as Draco. There is something quietly unsettling about watching
the man who played Draco as a boy play Draco as a father, and I recommend
sitting with that feeling rather than doing the arithmetic on your own age.

\img{/assets/NYCBroadwayLobby.jpg}{The lobby, pre-show, at maximum outerwear.}

\section{The eating}

More food than pizza was eaten. By God, a lot of pizza was consumed. The two
statements are both true and only one of them is surprising.

\img{/assets/NYCPizza.jpg}{Two of many. The green stripes were a good decision.}

Between the sit-down meals there is the other category, the one eaten standing
up on a sidewalk in a small circle while everyone insists you try theirs.

\img{/assets/NYCStreetSnacks.jpg}{The standing-on-the-sidewalk course.}
