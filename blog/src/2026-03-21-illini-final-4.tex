\begin{document}

\lead{A day trip, a quick alumni mixer, and Illinois handling VCU. ILL. INI.}

Drive in, watch basketball, drive out. There is a particular kind of good day that
consists entirely of one thing you were looking forward to, and this was one.

\img{/assets/MarchMadnessSign.jpg}{They inflate a large one of these and put it
outside, which never stops being funny to me.}

The alumni mixer came first, and it was exactly the format it needed to be:
orange and blue everywhere, a burger special, an ALUMNI placard on the table, a
pile of pom poms nobody was going to be shy about using later. Put two Illinois
grads in a room and one of them says ILL before anybody has agreed to start.

\img{/assets/IlliniAlumniMixer.jpg}{The mixer. Note the pom pom to salt shaker
ratio.}

\sep

Then the game. VCU never really got it going, and the shot of the scoreboard I
kept is the one with twenty points of daylight and three and a half minutes
left, which is the exact moment a tournament game stops being stressful and
starts being fun.

\img{/assets/IlliniVCUArena.jpg}{Pregame. Half the building in orange already.}

\img{/assets/IlliniVCUScoreboard.jpg}{71 to 51 with 3:28 on the clock. The good
part.}

\sep

And they kept going. All the way to the Final Four, which is further than I let
myself hope for in March, and then UConn.

Again.

I have decided not to write about that part. The day itself was perfect and I
am choosing to end the entry here, at 71 to 51, with the pom poms still in
somebody's hand.

ILL.

\end{document}
