Medium-Voltage Inertia-Less Isolated Converter

Center for Distributed Energy, Georgia Tech · Atlanta, GA
2024 – 2025Prototyped·ConvertersGT

Design and prototyping of a multi-level, medium-voltage, single-stage isolated converter — 800 Vdc to 2.4 kVac per slice. The single-stage approach removes the intermediate DC link that a conventional two-stage design needs, which takes out a large electrolytic capacitor bank and the reliability problem that comes with it.

Scope of the work

  • Topology definition for the multi-level medium-voltage stage.
  • High-frequency magnetics design, including the isolation transformer.
  • Control and protection implementation.
  • Hardware bring-up, firmware, and test and validation on the bench.
