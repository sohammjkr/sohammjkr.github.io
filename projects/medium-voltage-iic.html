Medium-Voltage Inertia-Less Isolated Converter

Center for Distributed Energy, Georgia Tech · Atlanta, GA
2024 – 2025Prototyped·ConvertersGT

The Inertia-less Isolated Converter (IIC) is an isolated DC/AC converter with no energy storage between its input and output. A DC bridge drives a high-frequency transformer with a quasi-square wave, and a bidirectional blocking bridge on the secondary connects that wave straight to the AC output.

This project took the IIC to medium voltage: a multi-level, single-stage version built from series slices, designed, built and tested at the Center for Distributed Energy.

Scope of the work

  • Topology definition for the multi-level medium-voltage stage.
  • Magnetics design of a medium-voltage, high-frequency power transformer.
  • Control and protection implementation.
  • Hardware bring-up, firmware, and test and validation on the bench.
