A single-stage isolated converter that unifies PV, battery and grid into one
conversion stage instead of the usual stack of separate converters. Built on the
inertia-less isolated converter, the prototype delivers 240~Vac from a 48~Vdc
source using a $\Sigma$--$\Delta$ modulation scheme.

\section{Why single-stage}

A conventional PV-plus-storage installation chains several converters together,
each with its own DC link, its own capacitor bank and its own losses. Collapsing
that into one isolated stage removes the intermediate storage element --- the
``inertia'' --- and with it the component that most often ends the system's
life.

\section{Approach}

\begin{itemize}
  \item Bidirectional single-stage isolated topology with an ultra-low-leakage
        transformer.
  \item Pulse density modulation ($\Sigma$--$\Delta$) for AC synthesis.
  \item Zero-voltage and zero-current switching across the operating range.
  \item AC-side switching, with PV, battery pack and grid all routed through the
        one stage.
\end{itemize}
