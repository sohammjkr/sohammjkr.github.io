The Inertia-less Isolated Converter (IIC) is an isolated DC/AC converter with
no energy storage between its input and output. A DC bridge drives a
high-frequency transformer with a quasi-square wave, and a bidirectional
blocking bridge on the secondary connects that wave straight to the AC output.

This project took the IIC to medium voltage: a multi-level, single-stage
version built from series slices, designed, built and tested at the Center for
Distributed Energy.

\section{Scope of the work}

\begin{itemize}
  \item Topology definition for the multi-level medium-voltage stage.
  \item Magnetics design of a medium-voltage, high-frequency power transformer.
  \item Control and protection implementation.
  \item Hardware bring-up, firmware, and test and validation on the bench.
\end{itemize}
