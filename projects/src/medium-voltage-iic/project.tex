Design and prototyping of a multi-level, medium-voltage, single-stage isolated
converter --- 800~Vdc to 2.4~kVac per slice. The single-stage approach removes
the intermediate DC link that a conventional two-stage design needs, which takes
out a large electrolytic capacitor bank and the reliability problem that comes
with it.

\section{Scope of the work}

\begin{itemize}
  \item Topology definition for the multi-level medium-voltage stage.
  \item High-frequency magnetics design, including the isolation transformer.
  \item Control and protection implementation.
  \item Hardware bring-up, firmware, and test and validation on the bench.
\end{itemize}
