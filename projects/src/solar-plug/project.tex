A plug-and-play microconverter for off-grid PV: an isolated inline converter
with onboard MPPT, aimed at Tier-1 through Tier-4 energy access at a cost that
makes sense in the markets that need it.

\section{The idea}

Energy access at the low end fails on cost and installation complexity long
before it fails on physics. Solar Plug puts the conversion and the maximum power
point tracking inline in a single small unit, so a panel and a load can be
connected without a designed system around them.

\begin{itemize}
  \item Isolated inline conversion with onboard MPPT.
  \item Direct PV inversion, targeting decentralised and off-grid deployment.
  \item Cost and power density traded explicitly against Tier-1--4 access
        requirements.
  \item Simulation and hardware prototypes.
\end{itemize}
