A low-cost submeter with integrated power-quality monitoring, built as my
undergraduate senior capstone. Circuit-level measurement of consumption and
power quality, with the data pushed off the device over Bluetooth or Wi-Fi and
displayed in a browser.

\section{Build}

\begin{itemize}
  \item ADE9153A and ADE9430 metering ICs for energy and power-quality
        measurement.
  \item ESP32 for Bluetooth and Wi-Fi data transfer.
  \item Real-time cloud telemetry and a web-based GUI.
  \item Over 24 hours of self-powered operation.
\end{itemize}
