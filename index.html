SOHAM MANJREKAR

Power Electronics · Atlanta, Georgia

Welcome to my page.

I am a founding engineer at GridTran , a start-up where we work on the bleeding edge of next-generation high-power DC infrastructure for AI datacenters.

My interests span power electronics, power systems, electric machines and entrepreneurship. I care about understanding the larger problems that the power and energy community face as a field, and about developing the engineering solutions that solve them to drive the energy transition and empower communities.

You can find my past projects and write-ups on the projects page, review my resume, or catch up on my latest activities on my Journal.
